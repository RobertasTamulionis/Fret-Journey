# Acoustic Guitar sample source

## Meta Steel Guitar v1.0.0

- Creator: Meta Creative Audio
- Source: https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Native source: real steel-string acoustic guitar, 24-bit mono WAV at 48 kHz
- Bundled material: twelve medium-velocity browser OGG derivatives rooted at MIDI 40, 44, 48, 52, 56, 60, 64, 68, 72, 76, 80, and 84
- Native recorded range: MIDI 40–84 (E2–C6)
- Playback range: MIDI 28–88; notes outside the native range use deterministic endpoint pitch shifting and are not represented as native extended-range acoustic recordings

The upstream repository and its sample manifest explicitly dedicate the recordings and metadata to CC0 1.0, including commercial use, modification, and redistribution.

High-Gain Electric does not add a separate sample library. It processes the existing CC0 Shinyguitar and low-eight-string recordings through a Web Audio high-pass, nonlinear waveshaper, cabinet low-pass, and dynamics chain.
