## 8-string guitar E1 (Freesound 557299)

- Creator: `minus_28_and_falling`
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/minus_28_and_falling/sounds/557299/
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Original recording: clean Drop-E 8-string electric guitar, open E1, 16-bit mono WAV at 44.1 kHz
- Bundled material: Freesound's public high-quality MP3 rendering of the CC0 recording
- Use: root MIDI 28, shifted upward by at most four semitones for MIDI 28–32

The Freesound source page explicitly permits copying, modification, distribution, and commercial use without permission.

No dedicated source samples are used for authored bends, slides, vibrato, hammer-ons, pull-offs, releases, dead notes, or palm-mute depth. Phase 2 applies deterministic gain, filtering, duration, and pitch automation to the picked samples where the TAB contains sufficient metadata; dead notes use a short deterministic unpitched percussive voice.
